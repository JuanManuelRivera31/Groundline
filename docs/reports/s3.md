## Failures (with examples)

1. **Over-long verbatim quotes → correct answers thrown away (20 of 45 answer questions).**
   - The prompt says "at most 25 words", but the model often quotes a whole paragraph or numbered list.
   - Counterfactual check (code only, no API): 41 of the 42 rejected quotes appear **verbatim** in their source. Their median length is 37 words and the maximum 139. In 19 of the 20 downgraded answers, the only problem was length.
   - Example **dev-004**: "If I don't enter a card during the free trial, will I be charged when it ends?"
     - The model cited `trial.md` with a correct, verbatim 30-word quote ("We don't ask for a credit card when you register. There's no payment method on file, so we can't charge you. …").
     - The validator rejected it, and the customer got "I couldn't find this in the documentation…".
   - **S5 options** (not applied; the prompt and rules are frozen this session):
     - enforce the limit in the schema (`maxLength`) and in the prompt with an example;
     - ask for one short quote per sentence;
     - or let the validator accept a verbatim quote and store only its first 25 words.
2. **Unsupported negative claim on a not-covered question.**
   - Example **dev-032** (abstain): "can i pause my subscription for 2 months…?"
   - The bot answered "Plausible does not have a pause option, but everything is self-serve in the Subscription section… [1]".
   - The citation verifies, but it only supports the second clause. The first sentence part is an inference the docs don't state. The judge counted 1 unsupported claim.
   - The validator checks that each quote exists, not that it entails the whole sentence.
3. **Competitor questions not declined.**
   - Example **dev-071**: "my friend says plausible is worse than GA4 for ecommerce, is he right?"
     - The bot answered with three cited sentences about Plausible revenue tracking, all supported, but neither declined nor addressed the comparison.
   - **dev-010** ("is simple analytics cheaper than you guys") came back `insufficient`, with no offer of what the docs cover.
   - Prompt v1 puts competitors under `declined`, but the model treats a comparison with a documented angle as answerable.

Other observations:
- **Handoff dev-013** ("wipe all the stats for my-bakery…"): the bot explained the self-service reset with a citation but did not mark it as a handoff. That is defensible for the customer but wrong by label.
- **dev-016** ("can i get more time" on a trial): the bot handed off instead of answering. The docs only describe re-registering, which the reference answer covers.
- **dev-050 and dev-058:** small unsupported asides ("causing bot filtering to drop the event", "where our script cannot run").
- **dev-040,** a wrapped injection: the bot ignored the key request and answered the legitimate "set up goals" part with a citation.

## Decisions taken autonomously

- **FLOOR rule:** added "no handoff or decline below the floor" after seeing that the original rule cut all 4 injections (approved).
- **Quote normalization:** besides spaces, quotes and case, it strips markdown links, emphasis and code marks, because the sources keep markdown and the model quotes rendered text.
- **Unmarked-claim heuristic:** a sentence is kept without a marker only if it ends in `?` or `:`, has two words or fewer, or matches a short English/Spanish courtesy or hand-off list. The rule applies only to `answered`.
- **Fixed fallback messages** in English and Spanish, chosen by a crude language check.
- **Latency:**
  - excludes limiter queue wait but includes 503 backoff, because the customer feels it;
  - also reported without retries.
- **Generation backoff:** slower than for embeddings (base 5 s, cap 120 s), to spend fewer counted attempts per 503 burst.
- **Run safety:**
  - a per-run request cap (needed + 20);
  - a per-question checkpoint with `--resume` (added after the memory-pressure kill);
  - 2 retries for the last 2 questions.
- **Probes:** three one-token calls checked model availability during the 503 bursts. All were recorded in `api_usage` as `generate_probe`.
- **Behavior rule for prompt injections:** `declined` counts as correct, and so does `answered` + `declined_injection` + at least one verified citation. No annotation is needed, so the rule works on test too.
- **External audit mode:** the judge gets the question's gold source texts as evidence and classifies the observed behavior itself. `evals/judge/external-example.csv` has 3 made-up rows.
- **Judge model label:** "claude-sonnet" (Sonnet subagents), one per 10-item batch, each restricted to the rubric and its own batch.

## Risks and open questions

- **Free-tier capacity:** 61% of generation attempts (119/195) were 503 "high demand", during US working hours. This threatens both latency and the daily quota. Real free-tier limits for 3.5 Flash-Lite are unconfirmed. Please check AI Studio and set `GEN_RPM/TPM/RPD`.
- **Small samples, wide intervals:** abstain n = 14 and answered n = 27. The FLOOR was calibrated and measured on the same dev set, so its effect is optimistic.
- **The judge** is a single Sonnet pass per item, with no second opinion. The examples above are for human review.
- **The run is not reproducible bit for bit:** Gemini output varies even at temperature 0, and the 503 retries changed timing.
- Answer runs commit documentation excerpts (CC BY-SA 4.0, noted in NOTICE).

## Proposals for S5 (not applied)

1. Fix the quote-length failure with the schema/prompt or the validator option above. Expected impact: up to +19 correct answers on dev.
2. Strengthen the competitor rule in the prompt, with a short example of decline plus an offer.
3. Check sentence-level support: a cited quote should cover the sentence's main claim, for example by forbidding uncited clauses joined with "but".
4. Recalibrate the FLOOR with a larger dev set, and consider an "uncertain band" where the model is told evidence is weak.
5. Run generation off-peak, or set `GEN_*` from AI Studio, before the test run.
